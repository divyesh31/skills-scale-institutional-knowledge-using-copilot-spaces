# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons manage communication between external stakeholders and the project team, ensuring alignment on expectations, requirements, and concerns throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Manage external communications and stakeholder engagement
- Document and clarify stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Escalate concerns and issues to appropriate project leadership
- Coordinate feedback loops from stakeholders to development team
- Ensure stakeholder satisfaction and alignment on project direction
- Maintain communication plans and stakeholder registers

### Goals
- Ensure clear, consistent communication with all external stakeholders
- Prevent miscommunication and unmet expectations
- Build trust and maintain stakeholder engagement
- Facilitate early identification of stakeholder concerns

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reports to**: Project Manager
- **Collaborates with**: Product Manager (requirements clarification), Developers (technical feasibility of stakeholder requests)
- **Coordinates with**: Communications Lead (messaging strategy)

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder meetings and status updates
- Requirements documentation and clarification emails
- Feedback coordination and escalation reports
- Stakeholder satisfaction surveys and check-ins

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads oversee quality standards and testing processes, ensuring that all work meets acceptance criteria and quality expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Oversee testing strategy and test plan development
- Validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria
- Manage quality metrics and reporting
- Coordinate with development team on quality issues and defects
- Identify and document quality risks

### Goals
- Maintain high quality standards across all deliverables
- Catch and resolve quality issues early in the development cycle
- Provide confidence in release readiness
- Reduce post-release defects and rework

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with**: Developers (quality implementation), Technical Architect (technical testing strategy)
- **Reports to**: Project Manager (quality status and risks)
- **Coordinates with**: Product Manager (acceptance criteria validation)

### Typical Communication
- Quality metrics and dashboards
- Test plans and defect reports
- Quality review meetings
- Release readiness assessments

---

## Knowledge Manager

### Role Summary
Knowledge Managers are responsible for capturing, organizing, and maintaining institutional knowledge, ensuring that lessons learned and critical information are accessible to the entire organization.

### Responsibilities
- Document project processes, decisions, and lessons learned
- Maintain knowledge repositories, wikis, and documentation systems
- Conduct and facilitate retrospectives and knowledge capture sessions
- Ensure information accessibility and searchability
- Create training materials and onboarding documentation
- Archive and retire outdated information

### Goals
- Preserve institutional knowledge and prevent information loss
- Enable faster onboarding and knowledge transfer across teams
- Improve consistency in processes and practices
- Support organizational learning and continuous improvement

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Collaborates with**: All team members (gathering insights and lessons learned), Project Manager (process documentation)
- **Supports**: Stakeholder Liaison (communication materials and stakeholder education)
- **Coordinates with**: Product Manager (feature documentation), Developers (technical documentation)

### Typical Communication
- Knowledge base articles and wiki updates
- Retrospective summaries and lessons learned documents
- Training materials and onboarding guides
- Documentation maintenance schedules

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical leadership and guidance, designing scalable solutions and managing technical dependencies and integration points throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and architecture
- Make and document architecture decisions
- Ensure solution scalability and performance considerations
- Identify and manage technical dependencies and risks
- Mentor and guide the technical team
- Review technical designs and provide technical oversight

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Minimize technical debt and rework
- Ensure smooth integration with existing systems
- Build team technical capabilities and expertise

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with**: Developers (technical implementation and mentoring), Quality Assurance Lead (technical testing strategy)
- **Advises**: Project Manager (technical constraints and risks)
- **Collaborates with**: Product Manager (technical feasibility of requirements)

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture decisions
- Technical reviews and code walkthroughs
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Team technical guidance and mentoring sessions
